Find out how many calories your body burns at rest with this BMR Calculator.

Your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) is the number of calories your body needs to maintain basic functions like breathing, circulation, and cell production.

Knowing your BMR can help you plan your diet, manage your weight, and reach your fitness goals more effectively.
