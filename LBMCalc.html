Discover how much of your body weight is lean muscle, bone, and organs.

This Lean Body Mass (LBM) Calculator estimates the weight of everything in your body except fat. By using your weight, height, and sometimes body fat percentage, it gives you a better picture of your body composition.

LBM is useful for athletes, fitness enthusiasts, or anyone working toward fat loss or muscle gain goals. Knowing your LBM helps you track progress more accurately than just using weight alone.
