Estimate your cardio fitness level with this VO₂ Max Calculator.

VO₂ Max measures the maximum amount of oxygen your body can use during intense exercise — a key indicator of aerobic endurance. This calculator gives you an estimate based on your age, gender, heart rate, and activity level.

Whether you're training for endurance or just curious about your fitness level, knowing your VO₂ Max helps you track progress and push your limits safely.
