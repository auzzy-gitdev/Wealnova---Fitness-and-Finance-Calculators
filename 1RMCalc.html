Want to know how strong you really are?

This One Rep Max (1RM) Calculator estimates the maximum weight you can lift for a single repetition of any exercise — like bench press, squat, or deadlift. Just enter the weight you lifted and the number of reps you did.

It's a great tool for strength training planning, helping you set proper workout goals and safely increase your performance over time.
