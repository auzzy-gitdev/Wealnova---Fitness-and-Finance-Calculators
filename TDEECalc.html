Understand how many calories you burn each day with this TDEE Calculator.

TDEE, or Total Daily Energy Expenditure, is the total number of calories your body uses daily — including everything from breathing and digestion to physical activity.

It’s a great tool for setting weight loss, maintenance, or muscle gain goals by showing how many calories you should eat based on your lifestyle.
